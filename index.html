12-month plan

Four phases. Each ends at a gate where DKG decides whether to continue.

  1. Phase 1: Qualify and open

    Months 1 to 3

    Real estate targets scored. India real estate offer agreed with DKG. At least three substantive discussions with developers or operators. One scoped real estate pilot. Index recommendation by Month 2.

  2. Phase 2: Scope and sign

    Months 4 to 6

    One discovery or feasibility engagement signed with a developer or operator. India working group for the Index launched.

  3. Phase 3: Deliver

    Months 7 to 9

    First feasibility assessment delivered. A second developer or operator in scoping. India input delivered to the Index on DKG’s schedule.

  4. Phase 4: Prove and convert

    Months 10 to 12

    A real estate project in design with named partners. India chapter agreed for the next Index edition. Month 12 review covering pipeline, revenue to DKG and the Year 2 plan with fee proposal.

Planning assumptions, reviewed at each gate

  • Real estate decisions at developers take 6 to 12 months from first meeting. In Year 1, a real estate pilot means a feasibility assessment or a design-stage project, not a built community.
  • If the Index’s first-edition data lock falls before Month 2, India’s input to the first edition is partial and the full India chapter lands in the second edition.
  • Year 1 revenue to DKG comes mainly from feasibility and pilot fees. Licences and project revenue fall mostly in Year 2.
  • Fifteen hours a week covers these two workstreams. A third workstream would need more hours or an added analyst.

Who owns what

I lead the India work. DKG keeps control of product, claims, delivery and contracts.

I own

  • The pipeline and its qualification
  • Conversations with targets, each led to a defined next step: a meeting, a scope, a pilot or a decision to drop
  • Opportunity briefs, partnership structures and pilot scopes, written with DKG’s teams
  • The decisions DKG needs to make, each with a requested date
  • A weekly written update and a review with DKG every two weeks

DKG owns

  • Product readiness
  • Scientific and clinical claims, and clinical governance
  • Delivery
  • Pricing approval
  • Contracting
  • The Index methodology, scoring, validation and publication

Definitions used on this page
Qualified target
An organisation scored on five criteria: strategic fit, project stage, access to a decision-maker, readiness to act within six months, and willingness to pilot.
Substantive discussion
A meeting with a decision-maker that covers scope, timeline and budget.
Scoped pilot
A time-bound project defined in writing, with a named buyer, the offering, delivery responsibilities, indicative price and success measures.
Warm access
An existing relationship with the decision-maker, or with a person who can introduce me to them.
